Research papers, tools and ideas

The group's research spans systemic risk and market microstructure, multi-agent coordination, governance and policy, and the philosophy of AI — approached as one question about how complex adaptive systems hold together when their parts want different things.

This page is the record: what is finished, what is with reviewers, what stalled, and what was abandoned. Software appears beside the paper it came out of. Work that is open rather than finished lives on Projects.
