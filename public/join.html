No money. Specified problems. Real authorship.

Dissensus has no money. That is the first thing worth saying, because it determines everything else on this page: there is no stipend, no compute grant, no travel budget and no institutional affiliation to lend. What the lab has instead is a set of research problems specified down to the blocking task, the data and code already on the table, a co-author who publishes where a result failed, and titles that cost nothing to give and are honest to hold.

01 · Roles

Three rungs, entered by doing the previous one

Roles here are research roles. There is no unpaid operations work, no unpaid communications work and no advisory board seat available by application. The ladder below is short on purpose: it starts at a task you could finish in a weekend and ends at a title you could put on a CV, and each rung is entered by doing the previous one rather than by applying.

I Open

Contributor

One named task. Five to twenty hours, once.

You take one specified gap from the open projects and close it. Blind-code a table, run one inference pass, rate a sample for severity, sweep a literature claim. The task is defined before you start and finished when the output exists; there is no ongoing obligation in either direction.

What you get
  • Named acknowledgement in the paper, or co-authorship where the contribution is substantive by the standard on this page
  • A dated written record of what you did, which is the thing that is actually useful when someone later asks
  • The output stays yours to talk about, publish alongside, or cite
How to enter

Pick a task below and say which one. No CV needed.

II Open · 6 projects

Research Collaborator

One project, carried to a result. Weeks to months.

You own a gap on a named project through to a publishable result rather than to a deliverable. That means the study gets designed, run, written up and defended, and if it fails it gets reported as a failure under the same inference standards a positive result would get.

What you get
  • Co-authorship, with author order agreed in writing before the work starts rather than after the results are in
  • Listed by name on the project record here, with what you own stated plainly
  • Standing to describe the work as done in collaboration with Dissensus
  • First refusal on the paid version of the role if the project gets funded
How to enter

Name the project and the gap you would take. Say what you would bring and what you want out of it.

III By agreement

Research Affiliate

Ongoing, cross-project, by mutual agreement. No hour quota.

The standing version of the above: you work across the programme rather than on one paper, you are consulted on design before things are built, and you are listed as part of the lab. It is not a job and it is not paid. It is entered after you have delivered something, because a title handed out before any work has happened is worth nothing to the holder.

What you get
  • The title Research Affiliate, Dissensus — usable on a CV, ORCID record, LinkedIn profile, or a paper's affiliation line
  • A signed confirmation letter on company letterhead stating the role and its dates, which is the artefact institutions and visa processes actually ask for
  • A dissensus.ai email alias, and listing on the About page
  • Standing co-authorship eligibility across the programme, and first refusal on paid roles when there is money
  • An affiliation for grant and fellowship applications where you need a host that is not a university
How to enter

Not applied for. It follows from work already done together.

03 · The other half

What the lab does not offer

Stated plainly, because finding it out later wastes more of your time than reading it now.

  • No salary, stipend, hourly rate or honorarium, for any role on this page, including the senior ones.
  • No compute grant, data budget, travel money, publication-fee cover or equipment. Where a task needs inference budget, that is named as part of the task, because the person who has it is the person who can do it.
  • No unpaid operations, communications, administration, fundraising or board work. Those are jobs, and the lab does not have the money to hire for them, so it does not advertise them.
  • No directorship, company officer position or Companies House filing for any collaborator or advisor. Dissensus Ltd has one director and that is a legal structure, not a reward.
  • No visa sponsorship, and no claim that an affiliation here substitutes for an institutional post. If a paid position or an institutional host is what you need, this is the wrong lab, and that is a reasonable conclusion to reach.
