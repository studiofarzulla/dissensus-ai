# Research Affiliate confirmation letter

Local working document. **Not deployed.**

This letter is the substance of the Research Affiliate title. A title with no artefact
behind it is worth nothing to the holder; a dated letter on company letterhead is what
institutions, funders, visa caseworkers and grant portals actually ask to see. It costs
the lab nothing to issue and is the most valuable non-cash thing Dissensus can give.

## Rules

1. **Issue only after work has been delivered.** The role follows a contribution; it is
   not granted on joining. This is what keeps the title worth holding.
2. **Always state the dates**, and always state that the role is unpaid and carries no
   company office. Ambiguity here can create real problems for the holder — employment
   status, conflict-of-interest declarations, immigration paperwork — and for the lab.
3. **Never imply an employment relationship.** No "employee", no "staff", no "position"
   with a salary implication, no line that could read as a job offer.
4. **PDF on letterhead, signed, dated, with the company number and registered office.**
   An unsigned Word file is not the artefact.
5. **Keep a copy** of every letter issued, with its date, alongside `advisors.json`.
6. **Renew or lapse honestly.** If the collaboration ends, the letter still states what
   was true for the dates it covers. Do not backdate, extend, or reissue with new dates
   for work that did not happen.

## Variant: contribution record (Contributor tier)

For someone who closed one task rather than taking an ongoing role. Shorter, and does not
grant a title — it records what happened, which is the useful thing.

> **Re: Research contribution by {FULL NAME}**
>
> I confirm that **{FULL NAME}** contributed to the Dissensus research programme
> on **{DATE(S)}**, specifically: {ONE OR TWO SENTENCES NAMING THE TASK AND ITS OUTPUT —
> e.g. "independent blind coding of the eighteen-row focusing-event table underlying
> Section 2.2, supporting the reported inter-coder agreement statistic"}.
>
> This contribution {is acknowledged in / carries co-authorship on} {OUTPUT}, {DOI or
> preprint link where one exists}. The contribution was unpaid and voluntary, and created
> no employment relationship of any kind.
>
> {SIGNATURE BLOCK}
